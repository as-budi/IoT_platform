Signing up

  1. Click Sign up on the login page and fill in the form.
  2. We send an activation link to your email address. It works once and expires after {{ limits.email_link_hours }} hours.
  3. Open the link and click Activate account. You can now log in.

No email? Check the spam folder. On the login page, Resend activation link sends a new one. New accounts get the User role; only an administrator can change roles.

Forgotten or changing your password

  • Forgotten: on the login page click Forgot your password?, enter your email address and open the link we send. The link expires after {{ limits.email_link_hours }} hours.
  • Changing: My profile → Change password. You are logged out of every other browser.

My profile

Open it by clicking your name at the bottom of the side menu. There you can:

  • change your first name, last name and email address (a new email address is used only after you confirm it from that inbox);
  • connect Telegram to receive notifications from rules (Telegram);
  • create an MQTT sandbox account (MQTT sandbox);
  • see and remove apps that sign you in with your account (Connected apps and API);
  • change your password.

Your username and role can only be changed by an administrator.

Roles

UserSees and manages their own devices, locations, rules and firmware, plus devices shared with them.
AdminSees everything on the platform and manages user accounts (For administrators).

Access to one device can also be given to other users with the roles Viewer, Operator and Editor; see Sharing devices.
